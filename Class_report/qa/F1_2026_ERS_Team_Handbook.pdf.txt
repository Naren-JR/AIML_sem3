Executive operating brief
Research question: To what extent can the latent electrical energy state of a 2026 Formula 1 power unit be inferred 
from publicly available car telemetry, with a transparent physical model, learned corrections and uncertainty 
bounds?
Core contribution to aim for: (1) explicit energy-flow model, (2) identifiability limits, (3) constrained physics/ML 
estimation, (4) comparison with meaningful baselines, (5) transparent limitations. Do not claim ground-truth 
battery SoC unless independent reference measurements support it.

3 | Physics: equations to implement
3.1 Longitudinal balance
aₖ ≈ (vₖ₊₁ − vₖ₋₁) / (tₖ₊₁ − tₖ₋₁)
F_req = m a + ½ ρ CdA v² + m g Crr cos(θ) + m g sin(θ)
P_w = F_req v
Sign convention: positive P_w means propulsion, negative P_w means net braking at the wheels. Grade is usually 
omitted initially only if the circuit elevation is unknown; the omission must be logged. Aero CdA is a lumped 
effective value that can change with active-aero mode, wind and car setup. Acceleration requires smoothing and 
should not be computed naively from noisy irregular samples.
3.2 Split of wheel power
P_w ≈ η_drv (P_ICE + P_K) − P_brake,friction  [propulsion/braking abstraction]
P_ICE and P_K cannot generally be separated from speed/throttle/RPM alone. Throttle is a driver request, not a 
calibrated ICE power sensor. In braking, regenerative braking and friction braking also cannot be uniquely 
separated without extra signals or assumptions. Hence the model must output a feasible family/range, not a falsely 
precise unique reconstruction.
3.3 Energy-store dynamics
Eₖ₊₁ = Eₖ − (p_d,ₖ / η_d) Δtₖ + η_r p_r,ₖ Δtₖ + wₖ
0 ≤ Eₖ ≤ E_window ;  p_d,ₖ ≥ 0 ; p_r,ₖ ≥ 0
Here p_d and p_r are defined at the chosen electrical/mechanical interface and must remain consistent with 
efficiencies; choose one interface in code and document it. E_window is the permitted usable energy swing, NOT 
necessarily total battery capacity. State of charge in percent is not identifiable without an independently justified 
full-capacity and offset mapping.
3.4 Regulatory constraints and implementation
The FIA 2026 technical rule set contains a 350 kW MGU-K electrical power ceiling and a maximum 4 MJ energy-
store SoC swing in the February 2026 technical issue; energy-management limits and modes have subsequently 
been amended. The project must parameterize the applicable limits by regulation issue, event, session, mode and 
lap rather than hard-code one recharge number. The FIA announced further 2026 energy-management revisions 
in April. Confirm the current Section C issue and any event-specific appendices before reporting a compliant result 
[R1–R3].

Structural issue: with P_w approximately known and both P_ICE and P_K unknown, infinitely many pairs can 
produce the same wheel-power trace. Likewise integrating estimated electrical power determines E(t) only up to 
initial E(0). A learned model does not remove this information deficit. Report relative energy change, feasible 
envelopes, or conditional estimates unless independent reference data anchors the state.
5 | Chosen mathematical estimator and chosen ML model
5.1 Baseline A — constrained physics-only energy-envelope estimator
For each time step, bound feasible electric deployment/recovery from wheel demand, conservative ICE power 
envelopes, braking opportunities and regulations. Propagate lower/upper feasible energy trajectories over 
plausible E(0) and efficiency ranges. Use constrained least squares (SciPy) to select a smooth representative 
trajectory; preserve the envelope as the primary uncertainty statement.
min₍p_d,p_r,E₀₎ Σₖ [λ₁(Δpₖ)² + λ₂(p_d,ₖ p_r,ₖ) + λ₃ slackₖ²]
Subject to energy dynamics, observed demand compatibility and FIA constraints. The product term is a soft 
penalty discouraging simultaneous charge/discharge; an exact mixed-mode or complementarity formulation is an 
optional refinement. A single smooth optimum is a regularized choice, not proof of uniqueness.
5.2 Baseline B — supervised ML proxy (when labels exist)
Choose HistGradientBoostingRegressor (scikit-learn) to predict a labelled ERS power proxy or energy increment 
from telemetry features. If no trustworthy labels exist, do NOT call it supervised ERS training. Synthetic physics-
generated labels are useful for pipeline tests but only measure recovery of the simulator’s assumptions.
pA_ML,ₖ = f_φ(v, a, RPM, gear, throttle, brake, s, segment, history)
Use time-lagged features and grouped splits by session/lap; avoid random splitting of adjacent samples. A 
regression output is projected onto physical constraints before energy integration. If available labels describe 
display graphics rather than physical electrical power, train/evaluate only against that display quantity.

5.3 Proposed primary hybrid — physics + learned residual + constrained smoother
Chosen model: gradient-boosted residual predictor + constrained energy-state smoothing. This is feasible within 
six weeks, interpretable, and easier to debug than an LSTM. The ML model predicts a correction to a rule-based 
electrical power proxy, NOT an unconstrained absolute SoC.
pA_raw,ₖ = p_base(zₖ; θ) + r_φ(zₖ, zₖ₋₁, …)
pA_feasible = Project_C2026(pA_raw) ; EA ₖ₊₁ = EA ₖ − Δt · pA_feasible / η  [signed convention]
For implementation use a single signed bus-power q: q>0 discharge, q<0 recharge; E_next = E  Δt[max(q,0)/η_d  − −
η_r max( q,0)]. Project the predicted q sequence jointly onto constraints using constrained optimization rather −
than independent clipping if lap-level energy limits couple time steps. If no real labels are available by Week 3, the 
primary deliverable becomes the physics-only feasible estimator; the ML module remains an explicitly 
unvalidated exploratory model.
5.4 Why not EKF as the main algorithm?
An EKF is useful only if the state has an informative measurement relationship; with energy absent from observed 
telemetry, a battery-only state whose measurement function does not depend on E is unobservable. Implement an 
EKF only after defining a defensible measurement proxy or reference trace. A constrained smoother/envelope 
method is a safer first implementation. Optional comparison: EKF with an independently observed energy proxy.
5.5 Training and validation
 Feature set: speed, smoothed acceleration, throttle, brake, gear, RPM, distance fraction, previous 1–3 samples, 
corner/straight category; tyre data is optional metadata, not a core feature.
 Target priority: synchronized real electrical energy/power trace > documented ERS display proxy > no 
supervised target. Never treat inferred labels as independent ground truth.
 Split by entire sessions or circuits. Fit preprocessing, imputation and model on training data only; keep at least 
one 2026 session held out where possible.
 Compare A: physics-only, B: ML-only (only with labels), C: hybrid; also compare trivial constant/zero-power 
proxy where appropriate.
 Metrics: MAE/RMSE for real references; constraint violation count, conservation residual, sensitivity to E(0), 
parameter intervals, across-lap consistency for unlabeled data.
 Leakage checks: no future telemetry in a causal deployment model; for offline smoothing explicitly state that 
future samples are used.

12 | Commercial hybrid-vehicle connection
Transferable: energy conservation, regenerative-braking constraints, constrained state estimation, uncertainty-
aware control and data/physics hybrid models. Not directly transferable: F1-specific power ceilings, lap budgets, 
racing duty cycles, aero assumptions and race objectives. Road vehicles often have battery current/voltage, 
temperature and BMS measurements; these improve observability and change the estimator design. In Week 6, 
discuss this distinction; do not claim experimental road-car validation.
